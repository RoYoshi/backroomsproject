DROP YOUR OWN SOUNDS HERE (mp3, ogg, wav, m4a, flac)

Name the files like this and the game uses them instead of the built-in synthesized sounds:

  death_hound.mp3     played when the Hound catches you
  death_smiler.mp3    played when a Smiler catches you
  death.mp3           fallback for either (if a specific one is missing)
  collect.mp3         picking up evidence
  step.mp3            footstep (played per step, pitch-varied) — step1, step2, ... are chosen at random
  hum.mp3             looping fluorescent hum (gets louder near lights, ducks during blackouts)
  ambient.mp3         looping background bed
  blackout.mp3        lights failing
  sting.mp3           dread sting when a monster is close

Numbered variants (step1.wav, step2.wav, death_hound_2.ogg ...) are picked at random.
Anything you leave out keeps the built-in sound. Delete a file to go back to the default.
Keep files reasonably small (a few hundred KB each; loops can be short and seamless).
