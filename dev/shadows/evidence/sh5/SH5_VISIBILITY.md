# SH5 — human-QA visibility correction

Continuation of SH4 final `3f12c4795531ca68404db7a86a8bf0ad5163098c` (tree `5893186598e07f16de24408b6ce5da52587a0475`),
verified on GitHub before any change (`sh4_final_remote_verify.json`). SH0–SH4 and their evidence are untouched.

**Why:** human QA of SH4 (`HUMAN_QA_VERDICT_SH4.md`) — *"It feels like the same game"* (PASS), *"The Shadows are VERY
Faint"*, *"The shadows aren't noticable"* (FAIL).

**What changed at runtime:** `assets/shadows-2d.js` only (`shadows-2d 1.1`): strength constants, and how a prop
shadow's strength is composed. No other runtime file changed. Caps, budgets, tiers, the lamp cache, culling, the SH3
layout fix and every safety rule are as in SH4.

## Why SH4 read as faint

1. **The strengths were restrained.** Measured in SH4 captures at MEDIUM, the strongest typical darkening of the lit
   picture (p90) was 0–0.15 in five of the eight scenes. The reception-counter flashlight shadow, the strongest SH4
   case, reached .33.
2. **A carried light's colour tint sits above the floor.** After cutting a light out of the dark, the overlay paints the
   equipment's colour tint over the beam (`source-over`). Floor shadows sit under it, so only part of what the module
   draws reaches the screen. `tint_probe.js` measured that share in the real client: of .69–.72 drawn behind the
   counter, .53–.55 reaches the screen, about 77 % (`perf/tint_probe.json`). Lamps paint no tint, so lamp shadows
   reach the screen as drawn.
3. **The SH2–SH4 captures understated the dynamic shadows.** Under the test clock's +1 µs-a-frame freeze, the 0.1 s
   caster fade-in of your light advanced only ~4 % a frame. The captures caught your light's prop shadows part-faded.
   `shots.js` now runs the frozen clock 2/3 s forward and rewinds it to the same instant before each capture. The
   overlay is unchanged across the tiers of every scene, which shows each capture is the same moment. The SH4 "before"
   captures below were retaken with this method, so before and after are compared fairly.

## Changes by class (`assets/shadows-2d.js`)

| class | SH4 (1.0) | SH5 (1.1) | intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| wall grounding | width 30 px, alpha .38, falloff power 1.7 | width 50 px, alpha .56, power 1.35 | wall bases read as grounded; a broad soft gradient, not an outline (V01) |
| your light: prop shadows | per sample `k/K` (k .6): ~.5 next to the prop, ~.32 in the tail, and darker at LOW than at MEDIUM | composed directly: next to the prop .8, tail .55, whatever the sample count | the clearest dynamic cue (V02); LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH equally strong, more samples = softer edges (V03) |
| other players' lights: prop shadows | k .5 | near .62, tail .4 | a little weaker than your own |
| lamps: prop shadows | k .6, presentation height 240 | near .76, tail .52, height 180 (shadows ~50 % longer) | visible in ordinary lit rooms (V04), restrained: lamps leave the fluorescent ambience |
| penumbrae (corners, pillars) | lamp .5; your light .55, others .45; half size 14 px | lamp 1; your light 1, others .75; half size 22 px | the hard edge the overlay cuts behind a pillar gets a readable soft side (V05) |
| entity shadows | player .26 / 70 px, hound .30 / 120 px | player .38 / 84 px, hound .42 / 140 px | grounded, still soft and partial; never a Smiler (V06) |
| baked-shadow thinning | ×(1 − .45·cos) | unchanged, and verified at the new strength (V07) | no doubled drop shadow |

The prop-shadow composition changed form (not just its constants). The tails of the K samples together remove
`tail × a` of the light. Next to the prop, the tails and one core together remove `near × a`, where `a` is the
caster's weight, solidity and baked-side thinning. Every shadow is still weighted per pixel by its own light's texture,
so it is still zero outside that light's cone and range.

Pillars keep the level art's own baked contact halo, a 6 px rounded rectangle at alpha .24. No grounding was added
under them, so nothing doubles.

## Measured (`visibility/`, generated by `report/mk_visibility.js`)

The same nine staged scenes were captured at OFF / LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH for SH4 (the SH3 commit's game files, module
1.0) and for SH5. Each is compared pixel by pixel with its own OFF capture of the same frozen instant
(`visibility_metrics.js`). Only pixels the player can see something on count (luma ≥ 18). The columns are p90 / p99
of the darkening, then the share of lit pixels darkened by at least 10 % / 25 %.

See `visibility/visibility.md` for the full table. At MEDIUM, from SH4 to SH5:
- p90 rose in every scene except the two Pillar Hall views, whose shadows are penumbrae only;
- p99 rose in every scene except `houndbo`;
- the share of lit pixels darkened by at least 10 % rose in every scene.

The scenes with little visible shadow area are the Pillar Hall views. There the big pillar shadow is the overlay's own
hard cut, as in v23.3.6, and the module only softens its lit side. The darkness overlay's mean alpha is identical across
the four tiers in every scene. `max` counts single anti-aliased sprite-edge pixels; the drawn composite is bounded by
unit test V08 instead.

## Bounds and safety (unit tests, `unit_tests.log`: 43/43)

The 35 SH1–SH4 tests pass unchanged. One exception: S02 and S03 told grounding strips from corner blobs by a hard-coded
"< 40 px". They now tell them apart by shape (a blob is a square, smaller than a cell), which is exactly as strict.

New:
- **V01 grounding:** .55 at the base, .22 at mid-width, nothing at the far edge.
- **V02 your light behind the counter:** .66–.72 at MEDIUM, strongest anywhere .73, nothing outside the beam.
- **V03 tiers:** LOW .70, MEDIUM .71, HIGH .71 at the same spot; 2 / 4 / 5 polygons.
- **V04 lamp shadows by the shelf:** strongest .69.
- **V05 penumbrae:** .43 a quarter of the way out, never above .46.
- **V06 entities:** hound .35, no Smiler shadow.
- **V07 baked-side thinning:** ratio .67.
- **V08 composed darkening:** in the busiest scenes, never above .85; .55 / .72 / .74 / .60 measured.

Browser checks `browser_checks.log`: 9/9. Among them:
- B02: the darkness overlay is byte-identical at every quality;
- B03: entity views are untouched;
- B04: the network messages are unchanged;
- B07: no Smiler shadow;
- B09: WebGL draws the shadows.

## Performance (`perf/`)

Polygon counts are unchanged: the same casters, caps and samples. The geometry covers more pixels: wider grounding and
penumbrae, longer lamp shadows. The SH4 report's open item was a −14 % fps on the mobile-like PILLAR HALL at LOW, from
one unrepeated run. It was measured again, three alternating rounds per tree and tier, back to back
(`perf/ab_mobile_dense.md`):

| | fps (mean of 3) | vs OFF |
|---|---|---|
| v23.3.6 parent | 0.883 | |
| SH4 OFF / LOW | 0.867 / 0.837 | LOW −3.5 % |
| SH5 OFF / LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH | 0.870 / 0.823 / 0.807 / 0.803 | −5.4 / −7.3 / −7.7 % |

The −14 % did not reproduce. Its LOW cost is −3.5 % (SH4) and −5.4 % (SH5). The extra ~2 points at SH5 are fill: the
same polygons covering more pixels. Software rendering pays for that on the CPU; a GPU would make it nearly free. The
module's own time there is 0.6–1.5 ms a frame on the 4×-slowed main thread, as before. The full matrix is re-run at SH6.
