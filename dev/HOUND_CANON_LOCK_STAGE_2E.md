# THE FAR BACKROOMS — HOUND CANON LOCK
## Reconstructed authoritative copy for Stage 2E

**Status:** Use this as the Hound canon authority for Stage 2E.  
**Continuity:** The Backrooms Wiki (Wikidot)  
**Primary source:** *Entity 8 — "Hound"* by Palico22, rewritten by 1000dumplings.  
**Source page:** https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/entity-8  
**Source revision observed:** revision 56, last edited 30 Sep 2025.

> This document distinguishes source canon from gameplay interpretation. If a feature is not supported below, do not present it as canon.

---

## Classification labels

Every Hound claim must be treated as one of:

- **CONFIRMED CANON** — directly supported by the authoritative Entity 8 page.
- **SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION** — a conservative reading of canon, but not stated literally.
- **GAMEPLAY INFERENCE** — a mechanic needed to translate the entity into the game.
- **UNKNOWN** — canon does not establish it; preserve uncertainty and do not invent a confident rule.

---

# 1. Identity

### CONFIRMED CANON
- Hounds are dog-like entities whose name comes from their canine-like behavior.
- They are humanoid in biology, with limbs adapted for travel on all fours.
- They move on all fours.
- They are highly aggressive / hostile toward humans.
- They can be temporarily intimidated by direct eye contact.
- Their attacks are physically powerful despite a sickly, skinny appearance.

### SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION
- The Hound should feel animalistic and physically aggressive rather than supernatural or calculating in a human-like way.
- A Hound can be dangerous without being omniscient or impossible to escape.

### UNKNOWN
- Human-level reasoning ability.
- Exact intelligence.
- Exact emotional state or motives beyond the described aggression.
- Whether all Hounds behave identically.

---

# 2. Confirmed appearance

### CONFIRMED CANON
- Humanoid body plan altered for quadrupedal travel.
- Long black hair growing on the head.
- Sharp claws.
- Extremely large mouth.
- Sharp teeth.
- Hair often obscures the mouth.
- Sickly / skinny appearance.
- Capable of powerful attacks despite that thin appearance.

### DO NOT INVENT
Do not add unsupported:
- glowing eyes as a biological/sensory power;
- extra limbs;
- horns;
- armor;
- exposed organs;
- supernatural body distortion;
- special markings;
- infrared organs;
- anatomical features borrowed from another Backrooms continuity.

If the existing game gives the Hound visible eyes for top-down readability, those eyes are **presentation only** unless separately justified. They must not create new canon senses.

---

# 3. Confirmed behavior

### CONFIRMED CANON
- The page states that Hounds become hostile when they see a human.
- Direct eye contact can intimidate them temporarily.
- That intimidation does not last indefinitely.
- The canon advice is to use eye contact long enough to get your bearings and escape.
- A growling Hound is a warning sign; the page advises remaining out of sight.
- Hounds maul targets / victims.

### IMPORTANT CANON AMBIGUITY
The page contains slightly different aggression wording:
- the description refers to provocation / an aggressive state;
- the behavior section says hostility begins when a Hound sees a human.

For gameplay, **visual confirmation of a human may be treated as sufficient for hostility**, because the behavior section is explicit. Do not use the ambiguity to invent psychic aggression detection or an unavoidable instant attack.

---

# 4. Locomotion

### CONFIRMED CANON
- Hounds travel on all fours.
- Their arms and legs are biologically adapted for quadrupedal travel.
- They are capable of pursuing and physically overpowering humans.

### UNKNOWN
- Exact walk speed.
- Exact chase speed.
- Acceleration.
- Turning radius.
- Jumping ability.
- Vaulting ability.
- Crawling through specific clearances.
- Stamina.
- Exact cornering ability.

### GAMEPLAY INFERENCE
The existing game's acceleration, turning, navigation, vault/crawl traversal, and pursuit speeds are gameplay systems, not canon facts. They may be tuned for believable play as long as they preserve the Hound's quadrupedal, physically aggressive identity.

---

# 5. Threat / attack behavior

### CONFIRMED CANON
- Hounds are hostile/aggressive toward humans.
- They maul victims.
- They have claws and a very large mouth with sharp teeth.
- They possess substantial physical attack power.

### SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION
- Close-contact attacks should feel forceful and animalistic.
- Pursuit after clear visual identification is consistent with the entity.

### GAMEPLAY INFERENCE
- Lunges, prediction, capture staging, or a specific attack wind-up may be used as game mechanics if they remain physically plausible and do not become new supernatural abilities.

### UNKNOWN / DO NOT CLAIM AS CANON
- Dragging victims.
- Feeding behavior after a kill.
- A specific tackle technique.
- A fixed attack combo.
- A fixed kill duration.
- Sadistic "playing" with victims.

# 6. Perception and senses

### CONFIRMED CANON
- Hounds visually perceive humans: the page explicitly ties hostility to seeing a human.
- Direct eye contact affects them, which also establishes that visual interaction matters.

### UNKNOWN
- Exact field of view.
- Exact sight range.
- Night vision.
- Infrared vision.
- Exact hearing acuity.
- Scent tracking.
- Vibration sensing.
- Ability to identify individual humans by sound.

### IMPORTANT
The current game's strong Hound hearing is an **existing gameplay interpretation**, not a confirmed canon fact from the Entity 8 page.

Stage 2E may preserve and polish that system, but must not describe "super hearing" as Wikidot canon.

---

# 7. Visible light

### CONFIRMED CANON
- The Entity 8 page does **not** establish a special attraction to light, fear of light, or light-based weakness.

### SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION
- A visible flashlight can expose a human because the Hound can visually perceive the human and the illuminated scene.
- A human who is easier to see can reasonably be easier to identify as prey.

### GAMEPLAY INFERENCE
- **Clear LOS to a human using visible light:** strong awareness/hostility is appropriate because the Hound sees the human.
- **A flashlight beam directly swept onto the Hound:** may be treated as a provocative attention event and can cause orientation/escalation.
- **Only a beam or illuminated surface is visible:** may create an uncertain investigation lead, but not knowledge of the hidden carrier's exact position.

### DO NOT INVENT
Do not turn this into:
- "Hounds are canonically attracted to flashlights."
- "Hounds can detect any active flashlight through walls."
- "Light automatically gives exact player coordinates."
- "Hounds are weakened or strengthened by light" unless separately approved from a source.

---

# 8. Infrared

### UNKNOWN
The authoritative Hound page does not establish infrared perception.

### LOCKED GAME RULE
For The Far Backrooms, Hounds **do not perceive IR**.

This is a deliberate conservative choice because canon does not grant that sense.

Therefore:
- IR OFF versus IR HIGH must not change Hound decisions.
- NV sensor state must not be AI evidence.
- Another player's IR emitter must not be AI evidence.
- Visible Hound eye presentation must not imply IR sensitivity.

---

# 9. Direct eye contact

### CONFIRMED CANON
- Direct eye contact intimidates a Hound.
- The effect is temporary.
- It can provide enough time for a person to orient themselves and escape.

### GAMEPLAY TRANSLATION
A Hound may:
- hesitate;
- slow its commitment;
- briefly hold position;
- back off slightly;
- otherwise show temporary intimidation.

But:
- eye contact must not provide permanent immunity;
- the player cannot safely stare forever;
- the effect must eventually fail / decay;
- breaking eye contact should remove that protection according to the implemented behavior.

Do not turn eye contact into a supernatural stun beam. It is temporary intimidation.

---

# 10. Hearing

### CONFIRMED CANON
- The page establishes that Hounds themselves may growl.
- It does **not** explicitly define exceptional hearing.

### GAMEPLAY INFERENCE
The project's existing hearing system may remain because it is already part of the established game identity.

If retained:
- sound must be evidence, not exact hidden truth;
- the Hound may investigate where a sound occurred;
- stale sound should lose confidence;
- wall / distance rules should remain physically coherent;
- sound must not silently reveal hidden velocity or exact post-corner position.

### 2F DEFERRED ISSUE
Player-specific footstep attribution remains a shared-intelligence problem for Stage 2F. Do not expand it in 2E.

---

# 11. Search, prediction and uncertainty

### GAMEPLAY INFERENCE
Canon does not specify search algorithms. For gameplay, the Hound may make conservative animal-like inferences from things it actually perceived.

Allowed:
- continue briefly toward last-seen movement;
- infer a likely path around a corner from observed heading;
- investigate a fresh sound;
- check a plausible nearby branch;
- pause/listen;
- choose the wrong branch;
- reduce confidence when evidence fails;
- eventually give up.

Forbidden:
- read the player's current hidden coordinates;
- read hidden velocity;
- know which post-corner branch the player chose without new evidence;
- secretly path toward the player while pretending to search.

**Core lock:** the Hound must be capable of being wrong.

# 12. Social / pack behavior

### UNKNOWN
The authoritative Entity 8 page does not establish:
- pack tactics;
- telepathic coordination;
- shared memory;
- alarm calls that transmit exact target information;
- hive-mind behavior.

### LOCKED IMPLEMENTATION RULE
Do not add new hive-mind knowledge in Stage 2E.

If multiple Hounds already coexist:
- each Hound keeps its own evidence;
- one Hound must not automatically receive another's exact target position or memory.

Any future social communication requires separate canon/design approval and a perceptible bounded mechanism.

# 13. Personality variation

### UNKNOWN
The page does not specify personality categories.

### GAMEPLAY INFERENCE
Small deterministic variation is allowed for game feel, such as bounded differences in:
- persistence;
- curiosity;
- hesitation;
- investigation duration;
- commitment.

Variation must not create:
- harmless Hounds;
- boss Hounds;
- radically different species behavior;
- hidden difficulty tiers.

All individuals must remain recognizably Hounds.

---

# 14. Knowledge model

A Hound may know or retain only evidence it legitimately acquires.

## ALLOWED
- current visual contact;
- last visual contact;
- last-seen location;
- movement heading observed while visible;
- sound evidence the game legitimately generated;
- visible-light evidence;
- anonymous investigation leads;
- evidence age;
- uncertainty;
- bounded encounter memory.

## FORBIDDEN
- current hidden player position;
- hidden velocity;
- future input;
- IR state;
- through-wall visual information;
- exact post-corner branch without evidence;
- another Hound's exact knowledge without an approved communication mechanism.

# 17. Stage 2E acceptance constraints

Stage 2E must preserve all of the following:

1. **Visual confirmation matters.**
   A clearly seen human is sufficient to create serious Hound hostility.

2. **Eye contact remains canonical counterplay.**
   It intimidates temporarily, not permanently.

3. **The flashlight is risky without becoming a magical beacon.**
   Clear sight of a lit human is strong evidence; indirect light is uncertain evidence.

4. **IR remains invisible to Hound AI.**

5. **Search remains fallible.**
   The Hound can predict, but it can also predict incorrectly.

6. **No hidden-coordinate reads.**

7. **Hearing remains a gameplay system, not a falsely labeled canon sense.**

8. **No new pack hive mind.**

9. **No change to Hound anatomy is authorized in 2E.**

10. **No arbitrary difficulty buff is justified merely by this Canon Lock.**

# 18. Canon self-audit checklist

Before accepting a Stage 2E behavior, ask:

- Is this directly supported by Entity 8?
- If not, is it clearly labeled as gameplay inference?
- Does it require information the Hound could not perceive?
- Does it accidentally give the Hound IR perception?
- Does it accidentally turn visible light into supernatural tracking?
- Does it preserve temporary eye-contact intimidation?
- Can the Hound still be wrong after losing the player?
- Did we borrow a trait from Fandom, Kane Pixels, Liminal Archives, or another continuity?
- Did we turn an existing game mechanic such as hearing into a false canon claim?
- Does this remain recognizably the Wikidot Hound?

If uncertain, preserve ambiguity and choose the smaller inference.

---

## Final canon summary

The authoritative Hound for The Far Backrooms is a **humanoid, quadrupedal, dog-like hostile entity** that is physically powerful, mauls victims, and can be **temporarily intimidated by direct eye contact**. Canon establishes visual interaction and physical aggression, but does **not** establish infrared vision, supernatural tracking, exceptional hearing, flashlight attraction, pack telepathy, exact speeds, or hidden-player knowledge.

Stage 2E may make the Hound more believable and intelligent through evidence-based prediction, search, hearing, light awareness, and uncertainty, but those systems are gameplay translations and must never be misrepresented as additional canon powers.
