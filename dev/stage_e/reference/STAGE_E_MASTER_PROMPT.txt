6. NAVSURFACE MODEL — LOCKED
==================================================

In spatial mode, extend the graph to surface-local nodes.

Conceptual node identity:

(navSurfaceId, localCellId)

A NavSurface is a connected walkable sheet/chart with unambiguous support traversal.

Requirements:

- two unrelated floors sharing identical XY MUST be separate nav surfaces
- a stair run may be its own chart connected to landings
- a ramp may be represented as a connected sloped sheet
- triangulation/support seams must not become thousands of artificial gameplay actions
- allocate cells only over occupied sheets/chunks, not every possible Z layer across the whole map
- stable deterministic IDs
- cells carry physical support references, clearance metadata and relevant material/cost inputs
- local cell centers have actual XYZ support positions, not inferred floor numbers

Flatten composite spatial nodes into the existing typed-array A* workspace at compile time where practical. One search must be able to cross local edges and traversal edges.

Do not introduce a hierarchical planner unless profiling proves it necessary and an explicit amendment is documented.

==================================================

   - continuous body-clear support

   - actual tread/riser sequence

6. VAULT
   - existing prop/traversal semantics plus real XYZ swept trajectory/landing
   - preserve current species eligibility and speed multipliers
   - physical collision may interrupt it

Every link needs more than two magic points. Treat entry/exit as portal regions/corridors wide enough for the actor and backed by physical geometry.

A query may discover geometry and apply existing capability rules. It may NOT invent a new species behavior.
