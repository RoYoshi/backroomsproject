P2 - the dark oval over the wanderer at a spawn (headless Chromium, SwiftShader, 960x540; every name and passcode synthetic; probe:
dev/pre-trial3-final-polish/tools/spawn_probe.js)

How the probe sees it: the page's clock (performance.now() and the animation frame's timestamp) is frozen the moment her fall starts
(a 4 ms watch in the page), so the game draws one moment of the 1.35 s fall again and again; she is stood about 70 px from the nearest
ceiling lamp (lit, as in the owner's screenshot - in the dark nothing shows on her); the world is frozen and she is in god mode.  Moments
.3, .45, .55 (falling: drawn 1.35x .. 1x), .66, .8, .95 (landed: the squash, settling), on BR-RoLE LOW, MEDIUM and HIGH.  (PixiJS draws
only when its clock moves forward: every moment is later than the fall is ever first seen, and the clock is nudged .3 ms after hiding a
PixiJS object; a first run held .2 when the fall had been seen at .22-.26 and its screenshots were stale - discarded.)

Cause, found in the source (C5, unchanged since the port):
1. The compiled client's arrival (src/core/client/game/client/arrival.ts, lineage B12) draws "its shadow grows as it falls": a black
   ellipse 28 x 16 at (x, y + 7), alpha up to .4, held .4 -> .16 after the landing, into view.death.foreground - a Graphics in the
   creatures layer, which render.ts adds to the world AFTER the wanderer (corpses, blood, debris, person, creatures, ...).  So the
   oval is painted over her body, hands and light for the whole fall and settle: the owner's screenshot.  Measured on P1 (= C5 here):
   the layer is above her at every moment on every tier; at the landing (66 %) hiding it brightens her body's middle from 35.3 to 50.7
   (LOW), 51.4 to 90.1 (MEDIUM), 11.9 to 17.4 (HIGH; a dimmer spot) - see spawn_probe_p1.json and spawn_*_before_after.png (top rows).
   After the fix the same comparison moves by at most 4.6 either way (the fresh render a hidden PixiJS object needs moves the view a
   little: noise, which is why this is reported, not judged).
2. BR-RoLE's silhouette for her (the cast's cut-out and the self-shading disc) was a fixed 18 px body, while the arrival draws her up
   to 2.25x (1.71x at .2, 1.35x at .3) and squashes her +-11 % as she lands: a smaller dark disc inside her body while she falls; the
   same for another wanderer falling in (mp.deaths.js draws peers the same way).

Fix (src/frameworks/br-role/client/br-role.js -> public/assets/br-role.js; the bundle is NOT changed):
1. While she falls and no death plays, BR-RoLE moves that foreground Graphics into the world just below her (same coordinates: the
   creatures layer has no transform) and puts it back at its own index when the fall ends, a death starts or BR-RoLE is off.  The
   ellipse and the dust rings stay: they are the fall's height cue, now on the floor under her.  Why not in the bundle's source: the
   gate's port and bundle steps prove the built client equal to the shipped one statement by statement and frame by frame (its scenes
   include the fall), with no allowance for a deliberate change; a presentation fix outside it keeps that proof whole.
2. Her silhouette follows the size she is drawn at (her avatar's scale on each axis, along her rotation), for her and for other
   wanderers; the cast's start cap too.  At scale 1 every number is as before.
