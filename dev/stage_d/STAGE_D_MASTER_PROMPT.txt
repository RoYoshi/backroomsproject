==================================================
8. TWO DIFFERENT VISIBILITY QUESTIONS — DO NOT MERGE THEM
==================================================

Stage D must preserve the locked distinction:

A. PHYSICAL VISIBILITY
What surfaces / actors could the local player's eye or sensor physically see through the actual Stage C geometry?

B. CAMERA OBSTRUCTION
What otherwise physically visible geometry blocks the top-down camera from viewing the local player / relevant local space?

CUTAWAY MAY CHANGE B ONLY.

Cutaway must NEVER alter A.

Example:

If a roof blocks physical LOS between the player and an entity above it, locally fading that roof for camera readability must NOT reveal the entity through the physical visibility mask.

Likewise, a real stairwell opening may allow physically visible lower/upper geometry across support identities.

10. LOCAL CUTAWAY
==================================================

Implement a local presentation-only cutaway state for the prototype.

{ worldEpoch, focusSpace, group states, fade progress }

- enter fade: ~0.15 s
- restore fade: ~0.25 s

These are presentation values, not simulation timings.

Cutaway rules:

- deterministic local candidate ordering
- fade/remove only explicitly cutaway-eligible camera-obstructing faces/groups
- physical geometry remains active for physics / physical visibility / light truth

- cosmetic fade must not create temporary entity reveal
- focus follows continuous Z through stairs/ramps
- do not wait for a floor index switch
- two observers at different elevations may have different local cutaways for the same authoritative world snapshot

Do not add a global "current floor hides all others" switch.
