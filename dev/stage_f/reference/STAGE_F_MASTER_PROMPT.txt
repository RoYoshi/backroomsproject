==================================================

inside a larger motor rewrite.

==================================================

Implement the common spatial pose history rules required by Stage F.

Histories are scoped by explicit world/lifecycle identities such as:
`(worldEpoch, entityId, generation)`

Reconnect, new world, respawn or authenticated teleport clears the affected
history/offset/slot associations.

documented change.

- supported segment: interpolate continuous path and reconstruct support height
- traversal: sample agreed continuous trajectory/progress

- never linearly blend unrelated stacked supports
- teleport/discontinuity never blends
- missing transition/unknown edge holds a valid pose rather than drawing through a slab
- ledge extrapolation stops if authoritative departure is unknown
- render FPS/camera/cutaway cannot change simulation or world authority
