SECTION A — SHARED EVIDENCE MODEL
============================================================

Preserve the Stage 2C evidence foundation.

Evidence should continue to represent observations such as:

- SEEN
- HEARD
- VISIBLE LIGHT
- ANONYMOUS LEAD

An observation should be able to carry appropriate properties such as:

- modality
- observation position
- time
- confidence
- uncertainty radius
- optional heading
- optional velocity if legitimately observed
- attribution state

Do NOT force all evidence into player targets.

A critical architectural distinction remains:

    TARGET EVIDENCE
    !=
    ANONYMOUS LEAD

An anonymous observation may cause investigation.

It may NOT automatically identify a player.

============================================================
SECTION B — CONFLICTING EVIDENCE
============================================================

Entities must now handle contradictory evidence coherently.

Example:

    Player A seen moving west 0.5 s ago
    loud sound heard north 0.1 s ago
    anonymous light seen east 0.2 s ago
    current target commitment remains active

The entity must weigh the information.

Do not implement:

    newest evidence always wins

Do not implement:

    closest player always wins

Do not implement:

    strongest raw number always wins regardless of species

Shared arbitration may consider:

- modality
- confidence
- freshness
- uncertainty
- current target continuity
- current state
- species-specific priorities
- whether an identity is actually known

Species logic must remain capable of interpreting shared evidence
differently.

The shared layer supplies evidence.

The species decides what that evidence MEANS.

============================================================
SECTION C — SOUND ATTRIBUTION
============================================================

Stage 2F must fix the existing issue where AI can effectively know which
player produced footsteps merely because the game event carries player
identity.

A sound occurring is not automatically an identified person.

Separate:

    SOUND SOURCE INTERNAL SERVER ID

from:

    IDENTITY KNOWN BY ENTITY

The AI-facing evidence model must distinguish at minimum:

    ANONYMOUS
    IDENTIFIED

If a sound cannot legitimately be attributed to a known person:

    entity gets a sound lead
    not a player target

============================================================
WHEN SOUND MAY BE IDENTIFIED
============================================================

Identity may be associated only when the entity has a legitimate reason.

Examples that MAY justify association:

- the entity directly sees a person produce the movement/sound
- visual continuity makes the source unambiguous
- an already tracked visible target produces the sound while continuously
  observed

Examples that DO NOT justify identification:

- footsteps through a wall
- running heard around an unseen corner
- a sound event containing sourceId internally
- a hidden player making noise outside sight
- server-side proximity alone

When attribution is uncertain:

KEEP IT ANONYMOUS.

Do not invent probabilistic identity knowledge merely to make AI stronger.

============================================================
SOUND POSITION
============================================================

Preserve existing:

- attenuation
- localization uncertainty
- range
- geometry rules

The AI consumes the perceived/noisy sound observation.

It must not silently recover:

    exact emitter origin
    exact hidden position
    exact hidden velocity

============================================================
MULTIPLE SOUNDS
============================================================

When several sounds exist:

- preserve each observation
- compare confidence/freshness appropriately
- allow uncertainty
- allow the entity to choose wrong

Two anonymous sounds from two hidden players must not magically become
correctly labeled Player A and Player B.

============================================================
SECTION D — HABIT MEMORY
============================================================

Implement only SMALL, BOUNDED, ENCOUNTER-LOCAL habit memory.

This is not machine learning.

This is not global adaptation.

This is not meta knowledge.

The purpose is to let an entity notice:

    "This perceived person has done this several times."

Examples:

- repeatedly peeking the same corner
- repeatedly choosing the same branch after being seen
- repeatedly returning to the same perceived hiding area

============================================================
HABIT MEMORY RULES
============================================================

A habit may only be learned from events the entity legitimately perceived.

Do not record hidden behavior.

Recommended conservative starting bounds:

- maximum 6 recent relevant observations per tracked perceived identity
- maximum 3 active habit hypotheses per entity
- approximately 30 second observation TTL
- require at least 3 legitimate repetitions before applying a bias
- bias should remain small, approximately <= 15% influence on otherwise
  plausible search choice

These are starting parameters, not an excuse to inflate difficulty.

Habit memory should BIAS.

It must never determine hidden truth.

============================================================
HABIT EXAMPLE
============================================================

BAD:

Player used left corridor three times earlier.

Fourth time:

Hound automatically knows player secretly took left again.

GOOD:

Player used left corridor three times while observed.

Fourth LOS break:

left becomes somewhat more plausible.

The Hound may still:

- check right
- investigate sound elsewhere
- be wrong
- lose the player

============================================================
HABIT RESET / LIFECYCLE
============================================================

Habit knowledge must not persist indefinitely.

Clear or expire appropriately on:

- encounter ending
- target becoming stale for a long period
- player death/respawn
- player leaving
- room lifecycle reset

Do NOT implement cross-life player profiling.

Do NOT store account-level habits.

============================================================
SECTION E — RNG STREAM ISOLATION
============================================================

This is a major Stage 2F requirement.

Current history has shared random-number consumption.

This means changing a Smiler's random calls can alter an unrelated
Hound's seeded outcome.

That coupling must be removed.

============================================================
RNG DESIGN GOAL
============================================================

Randomness should be deterministic but isolated.

Conceptually prefer:

    world seed
        │
        ├── entity Hound h1
        │      ├── personality
        │      ├── search
        │      └── behavior
        │
        ├── entity Hound h2
        │
        └── entity Smiler s1
               ├── personality
               ├── search
               └── behavior

Changing Smiler s1 random consumption must not change Hound h1.

Adding another unrelated entity must not change Hound h1's existing
random sequence.

============================================================
RNG IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
============================================================

Use deterministic seed derivation from stable inputs such as:

- world/session simulation seed
- stable entity ID
- subsystem/tag

Do not use wall-clock time.

Do not use rendering FPS.

Do not use Math.random in new AI decision code.

Do not solve this by pre-generating enormous random arrays.

Keep it lightweight.

============================================================
RNG SUBSTREAMS
============================================================

At minimum isolate random behavior by ENTITY.

Prefer further bounded subsystem isolation where beneficial, such as:

    personality
    search
    idle/behavior

Do not create excessive complexity solely for theoretical purity.

The critical invariant is:

UNRELATED ENTITY RANDOM CONSUMPTION MUST NOT ALTER THIS ENTITY.

============================================================
RNG MIGRATION
============================================================

Moving from the old shared stream may change seeded historical outcomes.

That is acceptable if documented.

Do NOT attempt to preserve an architecturally broken shared stream simply
to make old random numbers match.

However:

- distributions must remain bounded
- personality ranges must remain unchanged unless explicitly required
- gameplay tuning must not be changed merely because the stream changed

Where logic parity needs comparison, inject/fix equivalent traits and
observations rather than demanding identical old random histories.
