## why this seam
3H1's audit listed "client hum / dread audio -> a helper script" as a candidate.  The hum (B++ mp.js 887-984: a synthesized magnetic
ballast - buildHum, its crackle / thunk / strike sounds, its state, humUpdate) touches nothing of the network layer: it reads
window.__api and window.__sfx when the per-frame hook calls it, and mp.js uses only two of its names (humUpdate in the hook; buildHum
in __net.testHum).  Coverage first (d66b891): Stage 3H0's hum_dread scene plus the new hum_fixtures scene, 11 hum mutants caught.
The dread audio (initAudio, thump, burst, the drone) stays in mp.js, by decision: it registers its pointerdown / keydown listeners and
its MutationObserver at load, so moving it into a script loaded before mp.js would change the order of the page's listeners (which a
golden records, and which decides who runs first); and the dread layer is where 3H1 finding F-008 lives (not to be fixed by Stage 3H).
