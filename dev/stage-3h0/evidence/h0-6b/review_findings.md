# H0-6b: the second independent review of the final documents, and what was done

**What the review was.** After the H0-6 commit, a separate agent checked the final documents against the repository and the evidence. It had not seen the work being produced, and it worked read-only.

**What it checked:**
- every number in the report;
- the performance tables, against their JSON;
- the sizes;
- the human-QA claims that can be read in the code;
- the changed-files generator and the README.

**What it confirmed** includes:
- all commit and tree hashes, and every size and line count;
- the scope numbers;
- every suite, probe and mutant count;
- the function-equivalence totals;
- the contract counts;
- the real-socket and summon figures;
- almost every performance value;
- the human-QA code claims.

**What it found.** 26 points, listed below with what was done about each. All are documents or tooling text: no code changed.

**How each fix was checked.** Each was checked against the evidence before it was made. Two examples:
- the boot timelines in `evidence/h0-5/qa2/boot_*/boot_starts.json`;
- the exact values in `perf_det.json`, `perf_h0.json`, `perf_h0_server_r6.json` and `qa2/perf_ab.json`.
