# Stage 3B: prop and dressing canon audit

**Scope:** every prop and dressing family visible in the QA1 four-room slice, plus each replacement proposed for QA2. The rooms are YELLOW HALL, HUMMING ROOMS, BLACKOUT ZONE and PILLAR HALL.

**Status:** this is my audit for your review. You decide what fits Level 0.

## Sources

**1. Canon authority: the Backrooms Wikidot, "Level 0 – Threshold"**
- Link: <https://backrooms-wiki.wikidot.com/level-0>, revision 50, read 6 Oct 2026.
- The game's credits already link this page, and the project uses this wiki as its canon source.
- What it says about the environment (quoted):
  - "the sickly color of the wallpaper and mildew-ridden carpets";
  - "the grating sound of buzzing fluorescent lights";
  - "tight-knit, Berber-style carpeting", yellow in photographs but "closer to a brownish beige";
  - "persistent moisture";
  - the carpet is "seamless, consisting of a single continuous piece of carpet";
  - "randomly segmented rooms, hallways, and stairs".
- Named variants:
  - archway rooms ("pale walls with archway holes");
  - pillar rooms ("massive pillar rooms … in a lattice or grid pattern");
  - holes ("pits that lead deep into the floor");
  - blackout zones ("sections of Level 0 devoid of lighting"; "rough textures on the walls");
  - red rooms ("wallpaper peeling to reveal a crimson color underneath").
- Its overall tone: a "barren, sprawling maze" and a "vacant stillness".
- **It does not mention:** ceiling tiles, electrical outlets, wires or cables, furniture, desks, tables, counters, phones, papers, binders, glass or debris.

**2. Secondary only: the Backrooms Fandom wiki, "Level 0"**
- Link: <https://backrooms.fandom.com/wiki/Level_0>, read 6 Oct 2026.
- This is a separate community wiki, not the project's authority. I use it only to support an *interpretation*, never to establish canon.
- It describes:
  - "scattered electrical outlets";
  - a "drop ceiling";
  - "worn, moist carpeting";
  - "the back rooms of a dated retail outlet";
  - "indents in the level's carpeting suggest that furniture may have been present at some point in time, although this has yet to be proven";
  - a level "seemingly devoid of all life".

**3. The game itself (v23.3.6)**
- Physical props exist for gameplay, and their footprints are protected.
- **In the slice:**
  - counters L1 (YELLOW HALL) and L4 (HUMMING ROOMS);
  - the crawl-under table U1 (BLACKOUT ZONE);
  - crawl holes G1 (YELLOW HALL) and G4 (HUMMING ROOMS);
  - the nine pillars of PILLAR HALL.
- Stage 3B cannot remove these. It only decides how they look and what, if anything, sits on them.

> **Caveat:** Wikidot pages change; revision 50 was edited the day it was read. Where the authority is silent I classify conservatively. Nothing below is presented as canon fact unless the Wikidot article says it.

## How each family is classified

- **CONFIRMED CANON:** explicitly in the Wikidot Level 0 article.
- **SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION:** not enumerated, but consistent with the described architecture, materials and infrastructure. The secondary wiki or the level's own logic supports it, and it does not change the level's identity.
- **GAMEPLAY INFERENCE:** exists for gameplay readability or very restrained storytelling. It is a game adaptation, kept sparse.
- **AVOID / TOO SPECULATIVE:** reads as an ordinary office or shop, implies recent occupation, or adds conventional clutter.

## What this changes in QA2

- **Loose objects:** none remain on any prop or floor. The prop sets are all empty, and the renderer refuses a dressing kind whose class is AVOID. The class of every kind it can draw is recorded in `assets/level0_visuals.js` (`kinds`), so a future generator can only pick audited kinds.
- **HUMMING ROOMS:** no longer an office. Its identity is the hum:
  - yellowed lenses;
  - aged tubes;
  - outlets and junction boxes on the walls;
  - worn carpet.
- **YELLOW HALL** is the clean baseline. Its counter is bare.
- **BLACKOUT ZONE** is failed power, told through fixtures and decay only:
  - dead housings;
  - glass;
  - a fallen tile;
  - a burnt outlet;
  - damp, mildew, grime.
- **PILLAR HALL:** papered columns, damp at their bases, one fallen tile.
- **The carpet** is one seamless piece per room, as the canon says.
