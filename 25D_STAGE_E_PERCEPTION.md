# Stage E perception

The real Hound/Smiler brains consume spatial observations through the maintained
`20_senses`, `21_spatial`, `22_intelligence`, `25_light` and engine boundaries.
The physical query layer knows geometry; belief retains only legitimate evidence.

## Observations and uncertainty

Sight samples three target heights (eye, body midpoint, low/foot sample) from the
observer's real eye. Floors, slabs and walls occlude the physical segments. Range
uses root XYZ distance; the flat branch retains its original XY thresholds/FOV.
Nearby awareness does not bypass solids. Visible foot contact can establish a
geometrically inferred support candidate; an obscured foot leaves support unknown.
Observed velocity is calculated from consecutive visible poses, never copied from
hidden player velocity. Gaze requires observed facing/pitch and a physical eye ray.

Allowed spatial belief fields are `zMin`, `zMax`, optional observed `z`, bounded
`supportCandidates`, and `unresolved`, alongside existing position/velocity,
identity/anonymous attribution and timestamps. Memories retain last observed
elevation and inferred supports after loss; hidden player Z/support/floor/route
cannot refresh them. Predictions use remembered geometry. A reachable route choice
is a hypothesis, not a new observation. Up to four support alternatives are stored;
overflow becomes an unresolved region. Disjoint elevation evidence does not merge
because its XY uncertainty overlaps.

## Light, sound and live contact

Visible light evaluates the actual receiver eye elevation and physical occlusion.
World blackouts and existing visible-light rules remain. No receiver cache can
reuse a lower-floor value upstairs; this implementation has no receiver/light
cache. The camcorder has no visible emitter or AI light lead. IR-off/on paired
tests preserve complete entity decisions and RNG consumption.

Acoustic queries consider a bounded best route through air-space portals and
authored material transmission, with deterministic ties: at most 128 portals and
130 nodes. An occluded direct ray does not forbid an opening detour. The internal
source XYZ is used for propagation only. The brain receives apparent position,
intensity/confidence and a receiver/portal-derived vertical interval with bounded
alternatives, not exact source Z/support/floor/route. Sound without spatial origin
fails closed. Identity attribution still requires existing legitimate visual
evidence. Slab and opening cases preserve above/below uncertainty.

Live contact/capture/separation use full physical proxies plus intervening
geometry. Same XY through a slab is not contact; a genuine continuous seam can
permit contact without equal support IDs. The capture guard executes before RNG
or lifecycle changes. Stage G death/corpse migration is not part of this work.

Pack/group feasibility uses physical geometry and legitimate evidence. Sealed
stories cannot form an XY-only pack or share hidden player-floor truth. Existing
flat group/watchdog behavior remains under retained suites and frozen traces.

## Real-brain proof and bounds

The counterfactual tests run the generated AI and actual Hound/Smiler decisions.
After a common sight history, hidden player Z/floor/support/route/velocity and
destination differ. Complete enumerable entity state, memory Maps/Sets and counted
RNG streams are compared every tick. Both species match for 900 hidden ticks;
the real stair-branch pair matches for 1,173 ticks until legitimate reacquisition.
Positive controls diverge when new visible evidence arrives. Visible and hidden
camcorder IR pairs also match. No fixture brain scripts these outcomes.

Existing limits remain: 16 identity records, 8 sounds, 6 leads, 4 evidence entries
per identity, 6 habit observations, 3 hypotheses, and existing TTL/decay rules.
`spatial-performance.json` records peak per-entity counts, sight candidates/rays,
light queries, acoustic queries/nodes/portals and emitted surface alternatives.
No GPU/cutaway state is sensor authority. Physics remains fixed at 60 Hz.

The final acceptance log records all 65 underlying Stage E groups through eight
named Z10–Z17 gates. Z14 covers AI light/IR only; Z17 covers live entity physics.
Later presentation and death work remains outside this stage.
